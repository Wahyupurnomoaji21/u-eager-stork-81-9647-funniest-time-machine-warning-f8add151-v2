## About

A deadpan safety notice for a time machine, aimed squarely at the grandfather paradox — from a fresh angle: not refunds, not preventing your own birth, but the notice itself. It warns passengers to stay calm if their grandfather disappears, then undercuts the warning in the very last words: the reader has never existed, and neither has this notice. The joke turns a real hazard of time travel (erasing your own existence by erasing an ancestor) into bureaucratic calm, keeps a straight official face throughout, and saves the laugh for the final phrase.

Entry: **"NOTICE: If your grandfather disappears, please remain calm. You have never existed, and neither has this notice."** — **112 characters** (limit 120).

## Why this beats v1

v1 ("Do not prevent your own birth. Refunds are not available to passengers who were never born.") scored 43.65%, rank 1670/2212 in round 1. v2 keeps the same deadpan register but sharpens every criterion: the hazard is the grandfather paradox turned into the joke (the label erases itself), the punchline is the last clause rather than a mid-label refund gag, and no word is wasted — "please remain calm" does double work as official tone and as setup for the impossible calm of someone who never existed.
